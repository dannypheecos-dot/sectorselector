How to read the board

CosAnalyst research desk Sep 1, 2026 · ET

The score is arithmetic. Eleven SPDR sectors versus SPY. Same names every week. We recut Friday. Monday through Thursday is color on that rank — not a new map.

Published Tuesday 1 Sep 2026. Map as of Friday 28 Aug 2026 cash close. A CosAnalyst Research Project. SIMULATED RESEARCH. Not advice.

The formula

No black box. We do not print a 92 when the formula says otherwise.

score = clip(round(50 + 3.2×rs13w + 0.6×vs50d), 0, 100)

rs13w is closelook.net 63-session excess vs SPY, percentage points, price-only. vs50d is percent distance to the 50-day SMA (thetrading.tools sector-health, same close). Missing vs50d uses 0 and is footnoted — not invented.

Worked example · XLV:

50 + (3.2 × 13.1) + (0.6 × 4.5) = 94.62 → 95

Ticket rule

Every Friday close, rank the 11 SPDR sector ETFs by 13-week return minus SPY’s. Longs: top one or two only. Location must clear the 50-day and the 200-day. Rank says yes and the MAs say no → skip.

A #3 that still leads can be a HOLD. It is not a Sunday rewrite and not a new ticket. This week that is Energy.

Friday recut vs Mon–Thu color

The map recuts on Friday cash close. Monday through Thursday prints are color on the existing Friday rank. We do not invent a midweek score. We do not recut the 11-sector map midweek. If the tape sticks, we re-rank Friday.
