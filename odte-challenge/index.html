Challenge rules

Plain English

  1. Eligible underlyings only

    SPY, QQQ, IWM. Index ETFs. No single-name lotto. No crude. This book is not a sector-swing blotter.

  2. One official candidate per market day

    Official means the documented candidate that day — executed or not. If nothing qualifies, the day is logged as NO QUALIFYING ENTRY — CAPITAL PRESERVED. We do not force a simulated fill to satisfy the slot. A failed or watch-only name is documented as NO TRADE, not as a second official card. Sep 2 IWM is that row.

  3. No entry on a GEX target alone

    A GEX snapshot can name a destination. It is not a signal by itself. Tape has to qualify. GEX is a destination, not a promise.

  4. Defined risk. No averaging down. No widening stops.

    Size is set at entry. We do not add after a loser. We do not walk a stop further away so the trade can “work.” Hope-holds are a rule break.

  5. 1-lot at +50% must choose

    REALIZE PROFIT or PROTECTED RUNNER. There is no third option. At +75%, an unprotected hold is prohibited. A 1-lot cannot sit naked through a gift.

  6. Profit ladder is mandatory

    +25 / +50 / +75 / +100 / +200. Floors get published. See the ladder below.

  7. Time has a vote

    Expiration day uses published checkpoints. 4:15 ET is a hard backstop, not the working plan. Overnight is labeled overnight. It is not a same-day 0DTE entry.

Position-sizing and profit-taking

Defaults · set before the fill

Contract count is chosen before entry and written on the card. This challenge is on a 1-lot default until a later ticket says otherwise. Scale-out rules below are the published defaults for 2 / 3 / 4+ — they are not a hidden live book.

1 contract — this book so far

  • Entire risk is one defined-risk debit.
  • +25%: publish a premium floor (this ticket: $2.75).
  • +50%: REALIZE PROFIT or classify PROTECTED RUNNER.
  • +75%: unprotected hold prohibited.
  • +100% / +200%: trail the floor; harvest if the destination fails.

2 contracts

  • +50%: close 1 (realize). The remaining 1 becomes a PROTECTED RUNNER with a published floor.
  • +75%+: the runner still needs a floor.
  • Never add the second lot after a loser.

3 contracts

  • +50%: close 1.
  • +75%: close 1.
  • Remaining 1: PROTECTED RUNNER.
  • +100% / +200%: trail or take the last lot if the destination fails.

4+ contracts

  • +25%: optional first trim (1 lot) only if the tape is messy.
  • +50%: close at least 1.
  • +75%: close another.
  • +100%: harvest another. Leftover is a PROTECTED RUNNER with a floor.
  • Never widen a stop to “make room” for size.

Complete methodology

How a day gets on this page

What this challenge is

A 30-day public audit trail of index-ETF 0DTE and next-session tickets. One official candidate is documented every market day from 1 Sep 2026 through 1 Oct 2026. The page is a ledger, not a gallery of winning screenshots.

How a candidate is selected

Eligible tape is SPY, QQQ, or IWM. A GEX snapshot may publish a destination on the ticker — a price, not an option-premium percent. Entry still requires a qualifying tape. No GEX-only entries. If the tape does not qualify, the day is NO QUALIFYING ENTRY — CAPITAL PRESERVED. We document the watch as a NO TRADE row. We do not invent a fill so the calendar looks busy. Sep 2 IWM is that case — not a second official card.

How LIVE and SIMULATED stay split

A row is paper unless it says LIVE. This challenge, including the QQQ 707 put, is SIMULATED/PAPER. It is not a live Schwab ticket, not The Leap, and not the XLV sector-swing Track Record. Headline boards never mix the two books. Same-day 0DTE never shares a headline with overnight-managed-on-expiration.

How fills and marks work

Official P&L uses an executable bid/ask when one exists. If the bid/ask is pending, the card says so. Desk-indicated marks are labeled UNAUDITED and are not official returns. We do not invent premiums, MFE, or MAE. This ticket’s MFE/MAE stays an em dash until a verified bid series exists. Commissions and slippage are not modeled unless a row says they are.

How P&L is reported

Returns are per-contract, not portfolio. An open ticket shows no official P&L. Win rate, average winner, average loser, profit factor, and max drawdown are not computed from a single open trade. Those cells stay “—” or “open” until closed outcomes exist.

How time is recorded

Decision stamps are America/New_York, labeled ET. A PT source clock may appear as a note (Bookmap, a GEX snapshot) but the public stamp is ET. Alerts are labeled before or after the fill. This 707 put was alerted at entry.

How the ledger stays honest

The public JSON and this page are append-only. Status changes add a line. Prior OPEN is not deleted. Corrections are appended, never overwritten. Overnight stays labeled overnight even when it is managed on expiration day.

What this page does not publish

The Sector Selector Score recipe lives on the Friday map, not here. This challenge does not reprint scoring internals. It does not auto-post. It is not a paywall.
