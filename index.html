The rule

Four steps. If we change one, we date it. We do not backfill.

01

Rank

Every Friday close, rank the 11 SPDR sector ETFs by 13-week return minus SPY’s 13-week return. Longs: top one or two only.

02

Location

Long only if the close is above the 50-day and the 200-day. Rank says yes and MAs say no → skip. No “close enough.”

03

Structure

Debit call (or put) spreads on the ETF. 30–60 DTE. Max loss is the debit, sized in advance. No naked shorts. No 0DTE.

04

Exit

First of: drops out of the top 3 on Friday rank · time stop at ~21 DTE · 50% of max gain on the spread.

What would kill this. If eight weeks of paper cannot beat a coin flip after costs, the process is wrong, and we will say so. No P&L is claimed because none has been taken.

What this is

  • A weekly map and a short blotter. Cos Analyst writes the tape; this site keeps the book.
  • Paper tickets marked simulated. Open tickets show the debit paid, not a fantasy mark.
  • Weeks with no setup published as no ticket. That is the brand.

What this is not

  • A get-rich system, a win-rate brag, or a VIP room.
  • Day trading, scalps, or zero-day options.
  • Single-name lottery tickets or penny-stock promotion.
  • A signal service that changes the rules after a losing week.

Not investment advice. Options can expire worthless. Simulated results have limits: they omit fills, some costs, and the feeling of a real debit going to zero. Relative strength is a description of the past 13 weeks, not a forecast. Past performance does not predict future results.

Price-only series (dividends excluded) unless a total-return figure is labelled as such. Official SPDR performance tables lag to month-end; this board uses Friday session closes from the sources named above.
