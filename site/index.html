The rule

Four steps. If we change one, we date it. We do not backfill.

01

Rank

Every Friday close, rank the 11 SPDR sector ETFs by 13-week return minus SPY’s. Longs: top one or two only.

02

Location

Long only if the close is above the 50-day and the 200-day. Rank says yes and MAs say no → skip.

03

Structure

Debit call (or put) spreads on the ETF. 30–60 DTE. Max loss is the debit. No naked shorts. No 0DTE thesis.

04

Exit

First of: drops out of the top 3 on Friday rank · time stop at ~21 DTE · 50% of max gain on the spread.

What would kill this. If eight weeks of paper cannot beat a coin flip after costs, the process is wrong, and we will say so. No P&L is claimed because none has been taken.
