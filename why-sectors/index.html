Tailwind versus headwind

A leader on the Friday map is a tailwind. Capital is already concentrating there versus SPY. A laggard is a headwind. You can still be right on a name inside a weak sector. You are working against the tape.

Sector Selector ranks the eleven SPDR sector ETFs first. Then the free list sends one defined-risk way to play the leader. The trade is not the product. The map is the intelligence. The list is the audience door.

What this page is not

This is not a free trading course. It is not a dump of a proprietary formula beyond the V1 Sector Score already printed on the homepage:

score = clip(round(50 + 3.2×rs13w + 0.6×vs50d), 0, 100)

That score is a Friday-close ranking. It is not a validated 30–60 day forecast. The 30–60 day outlook is the structure horizon for the paper ticket (30–60 DTE). Rotation Score (0–100) is coming later. We do not invent it here.
